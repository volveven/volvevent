1. Introduction

Volvevent (“we”, “us”, or “our”) operates the OmniVoice mobile application (the “App”). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our App.

The controller responsible for data processing is Volvevent, Muhammet Hasan Gürcan, Bäckerstr. 8a, 84030 Ergolding, Germany, email: muhammethasangurcan@gmail.com.

2. Data Processing and AI Voice Cloning

OmniVoice is built with a strict “Zero Cloud” architecture. All voice cloning, audio processing, and AI synthesis happen strictly on your local device hardware. We do not upload, record, store, or transmit your voice data, audio files, or transcripts to any external servers.

3. Third-Party Services (AdMob)

While the core App is fully offline, the App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements (if you have not purchased the Ad-Free version). AdMob may collect and process certain information, such as your IP address and mobile advertising ID, to provide personalized or non-personalized ads.

Consent in the European Economic Area

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), the App uses Google’s consent mechanism (User Messaging Platform, UMP) to ask for your consent before AdMob serves personalized ads. If you do not give consent, AdMob does not serve personalized ads to you; non-personalized ads may still be shown. You can withdraw your consent at any time with effect for the future.

For more information on how Google uses this data, please visit Google’s Privacy & Terms site: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

4. Device Permissions

The App requires access to your device’s microphone and local storage exclusively for the purpose of generating the AI models locally. This data never leaves your device.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact us at our developer email address: muhammethasangurcan@gmail.com.

6. Your Rights under the GDPR

If you are located in the EEA, you have the following rights regarding your personal data under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):

  • Right of access (Art. 15 GDPR)
  • Right to rectification (Art. 16 GDPR)
  • Right to erasure (Art. 17 GDPR)
  • Right to restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR)
  • Right to data portability (Art. 20 GDPR)
  • Right to object (Art. 21 GDPR)
  • Right to withdraw consent at any time (Art. 7(3) GDPR)
  • Right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR), for example the Bavarian Data Protection Authority (Bayerisches Landesamt für Datenschutzaufsicht, BayLDA)

To exercise your rights, please contact us at the email address above. Since voice and audio data are processed only on your device, we do not hold such data about you.
